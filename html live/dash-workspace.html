Vetting summaryiFour classic impostor tests, run on the light curve itself: transit shape (planets carve a flat U; grazing binaries a sharp V), secondary eclipse (a glowing companion dips again at half-orbit), odd-vs-even depth (a binary caught at half its true period), and centroid shift (the dip comes from a neighbouring star). A ✕ means a false-positive fingerprint was found. All ✓ means no impostor signature — supporting evidence, not proof: confirming a planet needs follow-up observations.
